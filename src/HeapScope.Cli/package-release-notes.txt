4.5.0: GC handles of Server GC processes.

- Full dumps of Server GC processes with fewer heaps than processors lacked GC handles in every earlier version, so live objects
  could look unreachable and be left out by inspect, retained-by and paths. Open such dumps again: their index cache is rebuilt (4.5.0).
- heapscope-cli diff --allocated adds what was allocated between two snapshots that the app captured during one run that
  recorded allocations (4.4.0).
- heapscope-cli capture --kind gcdump keeps the names of the types when a trace of the process is recorded meanwhile (4.4.0).
- heapscope-cli sample has scenarios of allocation bursts (4.3.0) and of objects allocated and dropped (4.4.0).

All release notes: https://github.com/klundt-org/HeapScope/blob/main/RELEASE-NOTES.md
